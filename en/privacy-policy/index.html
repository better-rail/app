Privacy Policy

At Better Rail, we prioritize your privacy and are dedicated to safeguarding your personal information.

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used and improve it. It collects an anonymous device identifier, device and OS details, interface language, and usage statistics, stored on PostHog's servers in the European Union. We don't store your favorite routes and your search history, and we don't share any of this information with third parties.

We use Sentry to monitor crashes and errors. It collects device information, logs and diagnostic details, and records a session replay of sessions where an error occurred. Replays capture the app's screens only, contain no audio, and are not linked to you.

You always can opt out from this data collection by disabling the "Telemetry" option in the app's settings.

Better Rail Live

You have the option to get live updates on a specific train ride.

For this feature to work we keep the ride details during the time of the ride in an anonymized form. We also store logs to help us identify and resolve issues with the feature.

Where our data comes from

Train schedules and routes are calculated on our own servers, based on the Israeli Ministry of Transport's open public transport data (GTFS). Real-time information about delays, cancellations and platform changes comes from the Ministry of Transport's SIRI service.

Searches you make in the app are sent only to our servers, and are not forwarded to Israel Railways. These requests contain no identifying information, and we don't store them.
